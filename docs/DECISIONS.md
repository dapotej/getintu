# Product decisions

Rules Dapo has settled where the spec was silent or ambiguous. The code follows these; change them here first.

| Date | Question | Decision | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 21 Oct 2026 | "Yes" benefits (guest list, priority dining) | Scanned and logged at the door like any benefit. Each plan sets an allowance for them; unlimited is allowed (quantity left empty). | `benefits.quantity` (null = unlimited) |
| 21 Oct 2026 | "One per day per benefit" cap | Per venue: one use of a benefit per venue per day. The same benefit at a different venue the same day is fine. Matches the pilot rule. | `evaluate()` check 5, `VENUE_DAILY_LIMIT` |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Visits after midnight | Count against the night they started: 01:30 Sunday inside a window that opened Saturday is Saturday. Used for daily caps, offer dates and membership dates. | `serviceDate()` |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Crew shared perks | One allowance for the whole group, redeemable by any member, with party size recorded. Applies to the hosted night and to arrival meet-and-assist. | `benefits.shared_by_group`, `allowances.group_id` |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Re-scan within 10 minutes | Staff are told the visit has already been claimed and at what time. This check runs before the allowance and caps checks so it isn't masked by them. | `evaluate()`, `DUPLICATE_SCAN` with `claimedAt` |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Top-tier venues | Configurable in the back end: each venue has a tier, and each plan benefit lists the tiers it may use. Admin screens for both come in milestone 3. | `venues.tier`, `benefits.allowed_venue_tiers` |
| 21 Oct 2026 | Last night of a pass | A pass ending 10 January still works at a club at 01:00 on 11 January (the night it started). | `serviceDate()` |
| 21 Oct 2026 | One unit per person | Some benefits draw one unit per person in the party; others one per visit. Set per benefit. | `benefits.draw_per_person` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Benefit types | Replaced by an editable catalogue: categories → subcategories → service types, seeded from the "Category Taxonomy" tab. A plan benefit can sit at any level and covers everything under it; each venue offer delivers exactly one service type. Admins rename, hide, turn off and add entries in the console. | `categories`, `packages/db/src/taxonomy.ts`, `covers()` / `benefitFor()` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Credits | No credits. intu does not run a credit wallet. An offer is included in the pass, included or sold as an add-on, a paid add-on, or a member privilege. | `offer_model` enum |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Markup | Not entered. Worked out from an offer's cost and retail price. | `markup()` in the console |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Healthcare | The product is "Doctor on call" (C08-S02). "Doctor home visit" (C08-S03-T01) is a separate product. No health data is stored. | `taxonomy.ts` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Costs | Placeholder prices until real rates are agreed, marked with a "placeholder" flag on the offer. TEST offers use retail at cost × 1.5. | `offers.prices_are_placeholders` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Names | "intu" and "December Pass" stay. "Global Access" and "December Explore" are not used. | — |
| 22 Oct 2026 | December Pass gaps in the taxonomy | Added so existing benefits have a home: C01-S01-T03 Departure lounge visit, C04-S01-T02 Hotel pool and gym day pass, C04-S08 Golf with C04-S08-T01 Golf round or lesson, C06-S01-T03 Guest list and priority entry. 24-hour chauffeur moved to C01-S03-T04 (the sheet had a wrong ID). | `taxonomy.ts` (`added: true`) |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Partner pipeline | Not in the platform: deal tracking (status, priority, proposed offer, next action) stays in the internal sheet. A partner is the business; its venues sit under it, and each venue holds its hours, tier and offers. The console lists partners; a partner's page lists its venues; a venue's page (under its partner) manages its offers. | `partners`, `/partners/:id/venues/:venueId` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Plan cost and margin | Each plan shows what it costs to deliver and the margin at each price, if every allowance is used and at expected use. A benefit's cost per use is the average of the active offers it can be used at (venue tiers respected), shown with their range; a typed planning cost overrides it. Expected use defaults to 60% and is set per benefit. Unlimited benefits cost nothing when each use is free, otherwise they need a flat fee per member. Crew passes count each person's own benefits once per person and shared ones once. Fixed costs per member (concierge, operations, payment fees) are set on the plan. Figures are whole naira. | `costPlan()` in `packages/rules`, plan page |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Exchange rates for margins | Planning rates set in the console by founder or ops lead (naira per £1 and per $1), audited. Used only to compare foreign prices with naira costs; no paid rate service. | `fx_rates`, `/admin/fx-rates` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Kind of venue | A venue's category is a catalogue category or subcategory (Beach Clubs, Gyms, Airport Protocol), not a separate fixed list; the services it gives are its offers. Replaces the old list (beach club, hotel, golf…), which clashed with the catalogue. | `venues.category_id` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | TEST data | Rebuilt on the catalogue: the December Pass tiers from the concept doc, with 11 fictional partners and 14 venues (one or two per benefit), placeholder costs from the concept's wholesale estimates, and ₦42,000 fixed costs per member. Older TEST data is removed with `sql/reset-test-data.sql`. | `packages/db/src/seed-data.ts`, `sql/` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Pass codes (milestone 4) | The server signs a pass certificate for one device (membership, plan, dates, name, the device's public key), valid 36 hours so the pass shows with no signal. The device signs it every 30 seconds; a code is accepted for its window and one either side (90 seconds at most). Ed25519 throughout. A code is only ever approved once. | `packages/qr`, `evaluate()` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Backup code | A 6-digit code per pass per Lagos day, unique that day, checked online only. Five wrong codes in 10 minutes pauses manual entry for that staff member. *Assumption to confirm.* | `pass_codes`, `/scan` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Door staff sign-in | Venue login code plus a personal 6-digit PIN, unique among the venue's active staff. A shift session lasts 16 hours. Ten wrong PINs for a venue code in 15 minutes locks sign-in for 15 minutes. intu ops add staff and reset PINs in the console until the manager portal exists. *Assumption to confirm.* | `/partner/session`, venue page |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Offline visits that break a rule | Kept as approved and flagged to ops with the reason (spec: flagged rather than reversed). They draw on the allowance only if there's room. Visits more than 7 days old when they sync are refused. Whether the venue is paid for them is decided in milestone 7 (settlements). | `redeem()`, Visits to review |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Scanner look | Always the dark "Lagos after dusk" theme: it's used at doors at night. Members' photos show once the member app collects them (milestone 5); until then, initials. | `apps/partner` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Visiting services | Chauffeurs, home beauty and other services that come to the member are "visiting services": a venue without an address, whose staff use the same intu scanner on their own phones. They can only be scanned against a confirmed booking for the day (and for the booked offer, if one was set); one booking, one job. The allowance comes down at the scan when the job starts; if the job doesn't happen, ops reverse the visit, which gives the allowance back and cancels the booking. Offline, the scanner checks bookings it cached. | `venues.kind`, `BOOKING_REQUIRED`, `/admin/redemptions/:id/reverse` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Names | "Door staff" are **partner staff** and the "door scanner" is the **intu scanner**, since drivers and home-service staff use it too. Staff sign in with the venue's or service's **sign-in code**. (The internal role name stays `door_staff`.) | Console and scanner |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Plan codes | Made from the plan's name and kept short: filler words (pass, plan, the…) dropped, up to four words, words of four letters or fewer kept whole, longer ones cut to three, years to two digits. "December Pass Plus 2027" is DEC-PLUS-27; -2, -3 if taken. Nobody types one. They're internal labels; nothing depends on their format. | `POST /admin/plans` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Settings | Console-wide settings live under Settings, in tabs: Team and Exchange rates (spec: Settings area). Each tab shows only to those with its permission; /staff redirects to the Team tab. | `/settings/team`, `/settings/rates` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Database connections on Vercel | Queries aren't prepared (`prepare: false`): through Supabase's transaction pooler a prepared query could wait forever, which hung console requests until Vercel's 30-second limit. Also, a function instance starts on a fresh connection after 20 quiet seconds and gives up connecting after 10. | `packages/db` `createDb`, `apps/api/src/vercel.ts` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Forms in the console | Pages show information; anything that adds or edits a record opens in a side panel from a button (Add…, Edit, Review, Reverse…). Actions that need a reason for the audit log ask for it in a panel too. Plain yes/no confirmations stay as confirmations. | `SidePanel` in `packages/ui`, `ReasonPanel` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Catalogue entry pages | Each catalogue entry has a page: the venues and partners offering it or anything under it with their cost, retail and markup; the plans whose benefits include all or part of it; and what sits under it. Renaming, hiding and turning off happen there. | `/catalogue/[id]`, `GET /admin/categories/:id` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Scanner start screen and camera | Doors open on the camera (spec: full-screen scanner). Visiting services open on Today, their bookings for the day, with Scan one tap away. The camera rests after a minute with no scans or taps, and whenever the app is off screen, showing "Tap to scan", to save the battery on a shared venue phone. | `apps/partner` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Venue manager portal | Built after the member app (milestone 5), keeping the spec's order. Until then ops manage partner staff, hours and offers from the console. | — |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Member sign-in in TEST mode | Supabase phone auth with its Send SMS hook pointing at the API, which hands codes to a sender. Only the TEST sender exists: nothing is sent, and TEST numbers set in Supabase use a fixed code. WhatsApp and Termii plug in later. | `POST /auth/sms-hook`, `CODE_SENDER` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Member photo | A selfie with the front camera, about 600 px, in a private bucket served only by signed links (5 minutes for scanners, an hour for the member and staff). No liveness check until a provider is chosen. | `PUT /me/photo`, `member-photos` bucket |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Pass on the phone | The phone's own Ed25519 key in the keychain; the API signs a 36-hour pass for it, renewed whenever there's signal and under 30 hours remain, so it works offline for at least a day. | `apps/member/src/lib/pass.ts` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | "Included in your plan" | Decided by the API with the rules' `includes()`: the most specific benefit covering the offer, and the venue's tier. The scan still decides on the night (dates, hours, allowance, caps). | `GET /venues` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Map | Native maps with no key: Apple Maps on iPhone, Google Maps on Android (Expo Go supplies a key; a store build needs a free Maps SDK key). | `react-native-maps` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Trying the app | Expo Go, with each change to main published to the staging channel of intu's Expo project. | `.github/workflows/member-app-update.yml` |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Booking requests | Members request; the concierge confirms once the venue agrees, or declines with a reason. The concierge role gets `bookings.manage` (spec: Bookings, main role Concierge). | `/bookings` in the console |
| 22 Oct 2026 | Sign-ups outside production | People who sign up in the app on staging are TEST members. | `newMembersAreTest` |
| 23 Oct 2026 | Member app layout | Discover is a row per top-level catalogue category (Arrival & Travel, Food & Drink…), each with venue cards that scroll sideways; search, All/Day/Night and In my plan filter it, Map shows the same venues. Visits sit in their own tab inside Benefits ("What's left" / "Visits"). | `apps/member` Discover and Benefits |
| 23 Oct 2026 | How a benefit is used | Set per catalogue entry in the console ("How members use it"): show the pass at the door, book a visit, airport request, call, ask the concierge, or in the app. An entry with none set uses the one above it; top level defaults to the door. The app shows the matching button on each benefit's page. | `categories.usage_mode`, `usageModeOf()` |
| 23 Oct 2026 | Doctor on call | Unlimited and not counted. The number is the medical partner's own service line, kept on its venue record (a "service by phone or video" venue, with no scanner), not in settings. | `venues.service_phone`, kind `remote` |
| 23 Oct 2026 | Airport services | Requested from the app as a booking with the flight: arriving or departing, flight number, and time. The concierge confirms with the airport team. | `bookings.trip` |
| 23 Oct 2026 | Concierge benefits | Holiday planning, private chef and similar services are used by messaging the concierge on WhatsApp; nothing is scanned. | usage mode `concierge` |
| 23 Oct 2026 | What gets booked | Only packages and arranged services are booked ahead: a cabana, a club table, a private chef, services that come to the member, and airport services. Entry (gyms, beach clubs, clubs, restaurants) is a scan at the door with no booking, and a service line is called. Set per service type in the catalogue, so one venue can have both: entry at the door and a cabana to book. The API refuses a booking for anything else. | `isBooked()`, migration 0019, `POST /bookings` |
| 23 Oct 2026 | Doctor on call by type | The Call button follows the catalogue's "call" setting and the venue's service line, whether the venue is set up as a phone service or a place. | `venueActions()`, `callLines` |
